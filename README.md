Midnight Vault — a top-down stealth heist game. One thief crosses a museum at night, slips past three patrolling guards and their vision cones and three sweeping security cameras, steals the Star of Midnight diamond from the vault, and slips out the getaway exit before the alarm meter hits 100%. Smoke bombs and an EMP help, at a cost; bonus loot (a painting and gold bars) rewards the risky detour.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, throw a smoke bomb with Q or a mouse click, fire the EMP with E or a right-click. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty, $50 in USDC.
